SECURITY & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 29, 2026 | Application: Ai Era (Developed by Velocity)

1. OVERVIEW

Ai Era ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Velocity, is an artificial intelligence assistant designed to facilitate real-time voice conversations, high-reasoning problem solving, and device control actions. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed through your use of the application.

2. AUDIO & MICROPHONE DATA

Ai Era requests access to your device's microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) solely for the following purposes:

  • Transcribing spoken commands and queries into text via on-device Android Speech Recognition.
  • Displaying live audio visualizer waveforms during active voice input.

Strict Privacy Commitment: We do not store, record, archive, or sell your voice recordings to third parties. Voice recognition operates temporarily and exclusively during active user prompts.

3. DEVICE CONTROL & SYSTEM PERMISSIONS

Ai Era may request specific Android system permissions strictly to execute direct user instructions:

  • Camera & Flashlight: Used only when you command the app to open the camera or toggle the flashlight.
  • Alarms & Timers: Used to create alarms and countdown timers via the Android Clock system when explicitly requested.
  • Application Management: Used to launch installed applications (such as YouTube, Maps, Settings, or Calculator) based on your spoken or typed commands.
  • System Settings: Used to open relevant Android preference panels (such as Wi-Fi or Bluetooth) upon your request.

Ai Era will never access or execute hardware operations without your explicit user action.

4. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (GEMINI API)

When submitting conversational questions or reasoning tasks, text queries are processed through Google Gemini API models. All network transmissions are securely encrypted using standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

5. DATA RETENTION & SECURITY

Ai Era does not maintain remote user accounts, track geolocation, analyze background files, or transmit personal telemetry data to third-party advertisers. All conversational chat logs in the lobby are kept strictly in local memory during your session.

6. USER CHOICES & PERMISSION REVOCATION

You retain full control over the permissions granted to Ai Era. You can revoke microphone access, camera access, or other permissions at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > Ai Era > Permissions).
